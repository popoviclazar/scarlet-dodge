SCARLET
DODGE

One finger. Endless dodging.

Hold the screen to lift your glowing ball and release to fall. Slip through red gates, collect coins and survive as the obstacles get faster. Your shield switches on and off automatically, so watch its countdown and make every second of protection count.

Play Classic runs or the Daily Challenge that resets at midnight UTC, unlock new ball colors with coins you earn by playing, and compare your best score on the Game Center leaderboard.

Free to play

Scarlet Dodge is free and supported by advertising. There are no paid in-app purchases and no account is needed to play.

Requirements

iPhone with iOS 16 or later.
