Privacy Policy

Scarlet Dodge for iPhone · Effective date: October 28, 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you use the Scarlet Dodge game ("the app"). The app is developed and published by Lazar Popovic ("I", "me"), an independent developer. Contact: popoviclazar65@gmail.com.

Summary

The app does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location. Game progress stays on your device. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which may process limited device and usage data to deliver and measure ads. Game Center is optional and handled by Apple. I do not run my own servers and do not sell personal information.

1. Information stored on your device

Your best score, daily results, coins, unlocked skins, tutorial status and settings (sound, haptics) are stored locally on your iPhone. This information is not sent to me. Your device may include it in iCloud or computer backups according to your own device settings.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows banner, interstitial and optional rewarded video ads provided by Google AdMob. To deliver ads, limit how often you see them, measure their performance and prevent fraud, Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect and process information such as:

  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it;
  • device and app information, such as device model, OS version, language and an app-scoped device identifier;
  • ad interactions, such as ads viewed or tapped;
  • diagnostic and performance data, such as crash logs and app launch time.

The app does not request permission to track you through Apple's App Tracking Transparency, so it does not give advertisers access to your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), and it does not link ad data with data from other companies' apps or websites for tracking.

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app uses Google's consent tool to ask for your choices before ads are requested. You can review or change these choices at any time in the app under Settings → Ad privacy choices.

Google processes this information under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Game Center (optional)

If you choose to sign in to Apple Game Center, your Game Center profile and the Classic scores you submit to the leaderboard are handled by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. I do not receive your Apple ID or contact information. The game works fully without Game Center.

4. What I do not collect

I do not collect, store or sell your personal information, and the app contains no analytics service of my own, no account system and no in-app purchases. If you email me, I use your email address and message only to reply to you and keep it only as long as needed for that purpose.

5. Children

The app is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent me personal information by email, contact me and I will delete it.

6. Data retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you delete the app. Data processed by Google for advertising and by Apple for Game Center is retained according to their policies; you can manage it through your Google and Apple settings and the ad privacy choices in the app.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing of your personal information. Because I do not hold personal data about players beyond any emails you send me, most requests about advertising data should be directed to Google; I will gladly help you with any request at the contact address below.

8. Security

Information sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but I choose established providers and keep the app's data collection to a minimum.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
