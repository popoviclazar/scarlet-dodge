How do I play?

Hold anywhere on the playfield to rise and release to fall. Avoid red gates and the top and bottom edges of the screen. Passing a gate gives 10 points and each coin adds 5 points and goes into your wallet for skins.

How does the shield work?

The shield turns on and off automatically during a run. A countdown shows how long it lasts and turns orange in the final second. The shield protects you from red gates, but never from the screen edges.

What is the Daily Challenge?

Everyone gets the same course for the day. It resets at midnight UTC and your daily best is shown after each run.

How do I unlock new colors?

Collect coins during runs, then open Skins on the home screen. Coins can only be earned by playing; there are no purchases.

Why do I see ads?

Scarlet Dodge is free and supported by ads. A banner is shown below the playfield. After every three failed attempts, tapping TRY AGAIN may show a full-screen ad, and full-screen ads are at least 90 seconds apart. After hitting a gate in Classic mode you can choose to watch an optional video to continue your run once.

The video to continue did not appear

Ads depend on your internet connection, region and privacy choices, so a video is not always available. Wait a few seconds and try again. The run only continues after a video has been watched to the end. The game itself always works without ads, including offline.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

If your region requires it, open Settings in the game and tap Ad privacy choices. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Game Center leaderboard

Tap Leaderboard on the home screen and sign in to Game Center. Signing in is optional. Classic scores are submitted when you are signed in; Daily Challenge scores are kept on your device.

How do I reset my progress?

Your best score, coins and skins are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes them (unless restored from a device backup).
